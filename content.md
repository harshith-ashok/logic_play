Good morning everyone. I’m Kshama, and as the Marketing Head of Logic Play, I’m excited to introduce our club and share what we aim to build.

Logic Play is an AI-focused innovation club that provides students with a platform to explore, learn, research, and create impactful solutions using emerging technologies. Our vision is to build a vibrant AI innovation ecosystem where students can develop cutting-edge AI solutions, contribute to high-quality research, secure intellectual property, and participate in national and international technology competitions.

The club focuses on a wide range of domains, including Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Deep Learning, Generative AI, Computer Vision, Natural Language Processing, Robotics, IoT, and Data Science.

At Logic Play, we believe that learning should go beyond classrooms. We encourage students to work on real-world projects that address societal and industrial challenges while gaining practical exposure and developing industry-relevant skills.

The club also provides opportunities for students to contribute to research through journal papers, conference papers, and other academic publications. We aim to support innovative ideas through patents, copyrights, and technology disclosures, helping students transform their ideas into meaningful innovations.

Along with research and project development, Logic Play will conduct workshops, expert sessions, coding bootcamps, certification programs, hackathons, and innovation challenges. These activities will help students strengthen their technical knowledge, improve problem-solving skills, and gain hands-on experience.

We also aim to collaborate with industries, startups, research laboratories, and academic institutions to create more learning and innovation opportunities. Through teamwork and interdisciplinary collaboration, students will also develop leadership, project management, communication, and scientific presentation skills.

As the Marketing Head, my role is to help communicate the vision and activities of Logic Play, showcase the projects and achievements of our members, and build a strong presence for the club across different platforms.

Overall, Logic Play is not just a technical club—it is a community for students who are curious about AI, passionate about innovation, and interested in turning ideas into impactful solutions. We aim to build a strong and sustainable research culture that nurtures future innovators, entrepreneurs, and researchers.
