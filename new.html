The loop

Every puzzle runs on four moves.

Whether it's a five-minute daily or a forty-level campaign, the same loop is underneath. We design each beat on purpose.

01Move 1 of 4

Notice

Every board hides a rule. The first move is to look until you can see it.

02Move 2 of 4

Guess

Name the rule out loud. A wrong guess still counts, because now you can test it.

03Move 3 of 4

Test

Make the move that would prove you wrong fastest. Good players fail on purpose, early.

04Move 4 of 4

Click

The rule snaps into place and the whole board makes sense. That feeling is what we design for.

Then

The next board starts the loop again, one rule harder.
